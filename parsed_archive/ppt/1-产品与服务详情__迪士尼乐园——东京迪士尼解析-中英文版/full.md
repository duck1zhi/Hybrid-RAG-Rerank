**Disney Company did encounter a few unique cultural problems when developing Tokyo Disneyland.**

**迪斯尼公司在发展东京迪斯尼乐园时遇到了一些独特的文化问题。**

**The problem: how to dispose of some 250 tons of trash that would be generated weekly by Tokyo Disneyland visitors.**

**问题：如何处理一些250吨的垃圾，这些垃圾将由东京迪斯尼乐园游客每周生成。**

**The standard Disney solution: trash compactors.**

**迪士尼的标准解决方案：垃圾压实机。**

**The Japanese proposal: pigs to eat the trash and be slaughtered and sold at a profit.**

**日本提议：猪吃垃圾，被宰杀和销售利润。**

**James B. Cora and his team of some 150 operations experts did a little calculating and pointed out that it would take 100000 pigs to do the job.And then there would be the smell.**

**杰姆斯B.科拉和他的团队有150运营专家做了一个小小的计算指出它会带100000头猪来做这项工作。然后会有异味。**

**The Japanese relented.**

**日本让步了。**

**The Japanese were also uneasy about a rustic-looking Westernland. Tokyo’s version of Frontierland. “The Japanese like everything fresh and new when they put it in,” said Cora. ”They kept painting the wood and we kept saying, ’No, it’s got to look old.’” Finally the Disney crew took the Japanese to Anaheim to give them a firsthand look at the Old West.**

**日本也对质朴的westernland不安。东京版的边疆。“日本人喜欢一切新鲜的事物，”科拉。“他们不停地画木，我们不停地说，“不，这看起来过时了。”最后，迪士尼船员把日本海姆给他们亲眼看看古老西部。**

**Tokyo Disneyland opened just as the yen escalated in value against the dollar and the income level of the Japanese registered a phenomenal improvement. During this era of affluence , Tokyo Disneyland triggered an interest in leisure. Its great success spurred the construction of “ leisurelands throughout the country. This created an increase in the Japanese people’s orientation toward leisure. But demographics are the real key to Tokyo Disneyland’s success. Thirty million Japanese live within 30 miles of the park. There are three times more than the number of people in the same proximity to Anaheim’s Disneyland. With the park proven such an unqualified hit and nearing capacity Oriental Land and Disney mapped out plans for a version of the Disney-MGM studio tour next door. This time, Disney talked about taking a 50 percent stake in the project.**

**东京迪斯尼乐园的价值与美元的价值和日本的收入水平有了显著的改善，这一过程就打开了。在这个富裕的时代，东京迪斯尼乐园引发了对休闲的兴趣。其杰出的成功刺激了“全国leisurelands建设。这创造了日本民众对休闲的取向的增加。但人口统计数据是东京迪斯尼乐园成功的关键。三千万日本人生活在30英里的公园里。有三次以上在同一距离的人数到阿纳海姆的迪斯尼乐园。随着园区证明这样一个不合格的打接近容量东方土地和迪士尼映射为一个版的迪士尼米高梅工作室的隔壁的计划。这一次，迪斯尼谈到了在该项目中的百分之50的股份。**
